Problem: 
Taking a direct public transport bus from the Remera bus park to Byumba without passing through Nyabugogo.
Input: 
Packed luggage and Mobile Money (MoMo) for the transport fare.
Output: 
Successfully arriving in Byumba with all luggage.
Edge Case Handled: 
The current bus at the stage is already completely full.

Pseudocode:
START
    Arrive at Remera bus park
    Locate the direct Byumba bus queue
    IF the current bus is full THEN
        Wait in the queue for the next available bus
    ELSE
        Load luggage into the bus storage
        Pay the transport fare using MoMo
        Board the bus and find a seat
    END IF
    Travel to Byumba
    Alight at the Byumba stage and collect luggage
END

Why this is clear enough to follow: 
This pseudocode is clear because it moves sequentially through the physical steps required to complete the journey, starting from the required inputs. It uses a clear IF/ELSE decision block to handle the very realistic edge case of a full bus, ensuring the person knows exactly what to do instead of getting stuck. The START and END tags firmly define the boundaries of the process.
